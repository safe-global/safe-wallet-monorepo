|---|---|

**Proposal:** drop `isSafePays` and reuse the term that already exists in the fee layer — rename the prop to `isPaidFromSafe` (the `is` prefix matches the boolean-prop convention here: `isRelayAvailable`, `isLoadingRelays`). That leaves **two** intentional names with a clean split:

- `isPaidFromSafe` — UI/fee layer; the fact that matters is "the Safe funds the fee" (drives the `SelectExecutor` chip and the fee breakdown).
