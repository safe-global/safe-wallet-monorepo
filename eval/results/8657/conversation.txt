[gfendres 2026-09-08T06:21:27Z] Good catch, and correct on the mechanism. Fixed in 71e87d5, though not either of the two ways you suggested — here is what I found.

**Your diagnosis holds.** The Select tracks the highlighted row as a numeric index, set once from the selected row when the popup opens, and never re-derived when the list changes. Filtering renumbers the rendered rows into a fresh 0..N-1 range, so that stored index then points at a different row, or at none.

**Neither suggested fix was available as written.** There is no way to reset or control the highlighted option from outside: no `activeIndex` or equivalent appears in the public types for the Select root, item, or list; `items` is only a value-to-label map; and the item exposes no index override. **What actually fixes it:** the item's own `onFocus` writes its current index back as the active one.
