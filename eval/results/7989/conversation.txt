## 🍿 Summary

This PR reworks the safe selector inside workspace contexts to add an 'All Accounts' escape hatch, show a 'Safes in this workspace' header, retitle the footer to 'Explore other Safes', and filter the AccountsModal to exclude already-workspace safes. It also strengthens address-similarity warnings by bolding the first and last 4 hex characters in the tooltip, and extracts the modal’s data pipeline into a new `useAccountsModalItems` hook. Non-workspace flows are unchanged.

## 🏗️ Architecture notes

The new `useAccountsModalItems` hook centralizes fetching, workspace-exclusion filtering (via chainId + address match), grouping, sorting, search, and similarity detection. The modal and dropdown components now consume this hook; `SafeItemCard`/`MultiSafeItemCard` gained a `hidePinControls` prop for workspace context, and `ShortAddressWithTooltip` an `isSimilar` prop. The dropdown footer is always rendered and adapts its label, and a `DropdownWorkspaceHeader` component was added. No RTK endpoints or shared packages were touched; all changes are confined to the `SpaceSafeBar/AccountsModal` web subtree.
