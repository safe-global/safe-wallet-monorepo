The fix is correct and minimal. Root cause is accurate: `useFormContext().watch()` subscribes the component that called `useForm()` (the form root), not the descendant. Since `importedSafeResult` is not in `defaultValues`, the root only re-renders when its own subscriptions change, leaving `SafeAccountInput` with a stale `undefined`. Switching to `useWatch({ control, name })` subscribes this child via the field subject, which fires on `setValue` from `useImportSafe`. This also aligns with the sibling `ImportAccountFormView` (`apps/mobile/src/features/ImportReadOnly/components/ImportAccountFormView.tsx:26`).
