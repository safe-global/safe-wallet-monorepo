## Usage

<ComponentUsage component="tabs" />

Compose `TabsList` with `TabsTrigger`s and one `TabsContent` per value. Choose the look on `TabsList` with `variant` (`underline` or `toggle`), `tone` and `size`.

## Accessibility

<BaseUiReference component="tabs" />

The list has `role="tablist"`, triggers are tabs with `aria-selected`, and panels are linked to their tab. Arrow keys move between tabs; with `activateOnFocus` the panel changes as focus moves.
