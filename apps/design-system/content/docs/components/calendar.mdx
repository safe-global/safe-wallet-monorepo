## Usage

<ComponentUsage component="calendar" />

It wraps `react-day-picker` with Safe styles; pass its props (`mode`, `selected`, `onSelect`, `disabled`, …) directly. Put it in a `Popover` to build a date-picker field.

## Accessibility

<BaseUiReference component="calendar" />

Keyboard and ARIA behaviour come from [react-day-picker](https://daypicker.dev/guides/accessibility): the grid is navigable with the arrow keys, and Enter or Space selects the focused day.
