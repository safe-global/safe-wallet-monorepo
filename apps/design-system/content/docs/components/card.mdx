## Usage

<ComponentUsage component="card" />

Compose `CardHeader` (with `CardTitle`, `CardDescription` and an optional `CardAction`), `CardContent` and `CardFooter`. Spacing, surface and radius come from the `size`, `variant`, `surface` and `radius` props, so `className` stays layout-only.

## Accessibility

<BaseUiReference component="card" />

A card is a `div` with no role. Use a heading inside `CardTitle` (or `as`) when the card starts a section, and make interactive cards a real link or button rather than a clickable `div`.
