## Usage

<ComponentUsage component="sidebar" />

Wrap the layout in `SidebarProvider`, then compose `Sidebar` (with `side`, `variant` and `collapsible`), `SidebarHeader`, `SidebarContent` with `SidebarGroup`s and `SidebarMenu`s, and `SidebarFooter`. Put the page in `SidebarInset` and toggle with `SidebarTrigger`. On mobile it renders as a sheet.

## Accessibility

<BaseUiReference component="sidebar" />

Menu buttons are real buttons or links; pass `isActive` for the current page. `SidebarTrigger` has an accessible label, and the provider toggles the sidebar with ⌘B or Ctrl+B. On mobile the sheet traps focus like a dialog.
