## Usage

<ComponentUsage component="popover" />

Wrap the trigger in `PopoverTrigger` and the content in `PopoverContent`, with `PopoverHeader`, `PopoverTitle` and `PopoverDescription` when it needs a heading. Position it with `side`, `align` and the offsets.

## Accessibility

<BaseUiReference component="popover" />

The trigger sets `aria-expanded`. Focus moves into the popover when it opens and returns to the trigger when it closes; Escape and an outside click close it. `PopoverTitle` labels the popup.
