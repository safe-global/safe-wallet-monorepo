## Usage

<ComponentUsage component="radio-group" />

Put `RadioGroupItem`s in a `RadioGroup` and give each a `value` and a label. Use `value`/`onValueChange` to control it. For many options, use `Select`.

## Accessibility

<BaseUiReference component="radio-group" />

The group has `role="radiogroup"`. Tab moves into and out of the group, and the arrow keys move the selection between options. Give the group a label, for example with `FieldSet` and `FieldLegend`.
